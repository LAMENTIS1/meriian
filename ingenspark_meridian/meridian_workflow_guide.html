1
Data Input & Ingestion Layer
What this is used for: Ingesting, reshaping, and rigorously validating rectangular panel datasets (across Geos and Weeks) for media impressions, spend, reach/frequency, controls, and baseline KPI targets.
  • Loaders: Ingestion pipelines for pandas DataFrames, CSVs, xarray Datasets, and raw NumPy arrays.
  • CoordToColumns: Standardized column-to-dimension mapping schema for automated ETL.
  • Validation: Shape checking, time-step continuity validation, and non-negative spend/impression verification.
  • Channel Types: Direct media channels, Reach & Frequency (RF) channels, organic media, non-media controls, and population weights.
  • Currency: Optional explicit ISO currency coding for financial aggregations.
meridian.data.InputData CsvDataLoader DataFrameDataLoader XrDatasetDataLoader CoordToColumns
2
Model Specification Layer
What this is used for: Formulating the structural econometric equations, Adstock carryover decay, Hill saturation curves, time-varying baseline trends, and hierarchical geo structures.
  • Adstock Decay: Geometric or binomial decay curves configured per channel.
  • Saturation Function: Non-linear Hill curves or linear responses per channel.
  • Transformation Ordering: Ability to apply Hill saturation before or after Adstock carryover.
  • Knots (Baseline): Time-varying baseline splines with Automatic Knot Selection (AKS).
  • Hierarchical Geo Effects: Geo-level shrinkage using Normal or Log-Normal media distribution effects.
  • Changepoints: New time-varying media effectiveness changepoints to model structural shifts.
meridian.model.spec.ModelSpec SaturationType adstock_hill knots.py changepoints.py
3
Priors and Experimental Calibration
What this is used for: Injecting business intuition, historical benchmarks, and randomized geo-experiment ground-truth into the model prior to fitting.
  • Media Prior Types: Four flexible formulations: ROI, marginal ROI (mROI), contribution share, and direct regression coefficient.
  • Custom Prior Overrides: Configure custom parametric priors (Lognormal, Half-Normal, Beta, Normal) per channel.
  • Experiment Calibration: CalibrationBuilder ingests incrementality test results (e.g. Meridian GeoX lift tests) with recency, duration, and spend adjustments.
  • ROI Calibration Periods: Restrict calibration constraints strictly to the test experiment's execution window.
meridian.model.prior_distribution.PriorDistribution CalibrationSpec ChannelCalibrationSpec
4
Holdout Splits (Validation)
What this is used for: Creating train/validation splits to test out-of-sample predictive accuracy while preserving media adstock carryover continuity.
  • Geo & Date Holdouts: Exclude specific DMAs or time intervals from likelihood computation.
  • Balanced Random Holdouts: Randomly mask cells across time while maintaining regional balance.
  • Adstock Preservation: Media spend is retained across holdout periods so cumulative adstock carry-over math remains accurate.
meridian.model.spec.GeoHoldoutSpec meridian.model.spec.RandomHoldoutSpec HoldoutSpec
5
Probabilistic MCMC Sampling Engine
What this is used for: Drawing full Bayesian posterior distributions for all model parameters using No-U-Turn Sampler (NUTS) across GPU/CPU hardware.
  • Prior Sampling: sample_prior() validates prior assumptions and checks implied outcomes before fitting.
  • Posterior Sampling: sample_posterior() runs multi-chain NUTS with adaptive step sizes.
  • Progress & Thinning: Live TQDM progress indicators and posterior thinning to conserve memory.
  • Execution Backends: JAX engine (default high-performance backend) and TensorFlow Probability.
meridian.model.Meridian posterior_sampler.py prior_sampler.py
6
Model Review & Diagnostic Health Checks
What this is used for: Assessing mathematical convergence, goodness of fit, and potential econometric biases before presenting results to stakeholders.
  • ModelReviewer Health Score: Categorizes model health as PASS, REVIEW, or FAIL.
  • Convergence Checks: Gelman-Rubin R-hat and Effective Sample Size (ESS).
  • Fit Metrics: Bayesian posterior predictive p-values, R², MAPE, and weighted MAPE.
  • Econometric Checks: Prior-to-posterior shift, negative baseline probability, implausible ROI checks, and control multicollinearity.
  • Pre-fit EDA Engine: Invariability tests, Variance Inflation Factor (VIF), and population correlation.
meridian.analysis.review.ModelReviewer eda.py health_summary()
7
Analysis & Outcome Extraction Layer
What this is used for: Translating sampled posterior parameters into actionable business metrics: incremental revenue, ROI, mROI, and diminishing returns.
  • Incremental Conversions/Revenue: Posterior distributions of channel incremental outcome.
  • Efficiency Metrics: Return on Investment (ROI), Marginal ROI (mROI), and Cost per Incremental KPI (CPIK).
  • Full Credible Intervals: Explicit uncertainty bounds (e.g. 90% credible intervals) on every metric.
  • Flexible Slicing: Compute national or geo-level metrics over custom time windows.
  • Counterfactuals: Evaluate KPI outcomes under hypothetical media interventions.
meridian.analysis.analyzer.Analyzer get_incremental_kpi() expected_outcome()
8
Visualization & Diagnostic Plotting
What this is used for: Generating interactive, publication-grade visual diagnostics and charts using Altair / Vega-Lite.
  • ModelFit: Actual vs. predicted fit curves over time across individual geos or nationally.
  • ModelDiagnostics: Visual R-hat distributions, chain trace plots, and prior vs. posterior shifts.
  • MediaEffects: Hill saturation curves, diminishing returns, and geometric Adstock decay half-lives.
  • MediaSummary: Waterfall contribution charts, ROI comparisons, and spend-to-revenue ratios.
  • ReachAndFrequency: Optimal frequency saturation curves for RF channels.
meridian.analysis.visualizer.Visualizer ModelFit MediaEffects ReachAndFrequency
9
Budget Optimizer & Scenario Planning
What this is used for: Calculating mathematical optimal spend allocations across marketing channels to maximize revenue or target specific ROI thresholds.
  • Fixed-Budget Optimization: Reallocates an existing or scaled budget to maximize incremental outcome.
  • Flexible-Budget Optimization: Determines the optimal total budget required to hit a target ROI or mROI hurdle rate.
  • Spend Constraints: Imposes upper and lower bounds (relative or absolute) per channel to prevent unrealistic shifts.
  • RF Frequency Optimization: Solves for optimal target frequency for digital reach campaigns.
  • Spend Grids: Generates multi-dimensional response grids for interactive what-if explorations.
meridian.analysis.optimizer.BudgetOptimizer FixedBudgetScenario FlexibleBudgetScenario OptimizationGrid

Advanced Full-Funnel Modeling (Custom Extension)
What this is used for: Connecting upper-funnel branding campaigns to intermediate KPIs (e.g. site visits, brand search) and flowing those into final sales revenue.
  • Two-Stage Architecture: Upper-funnel model drives brand metrics; lower-funnel model drives sales with upper metrics as controls.
  • Implementation: Built via user custom class (AnalyzerFullFunnel) using Meridian's modular primitives.

Key Production Considerations & Limitations

• Aggregated Data: Built for geo-temporal aggregate data, not individual user-level tracking.
• Hardware Requirement: MCMC sampling with NUTS benefits heavily from a T4/V100/A100 GPU.
• Non-Revenue KPIs: Require custom priors or revenue-per-unit mappings to avoid falling back to total contribution priors.
